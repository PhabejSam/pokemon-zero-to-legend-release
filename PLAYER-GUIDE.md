## Start with an unlikely partner

Choose your original partner in Professor Elm's lab in New Bark Town. First-time players start a new game; do not import a save from Emerald or another hack.

| Original starter | Starting moves, in slot order |
| --- | --- |
| Magikarp | Splash, Tackle, Aqua Jet, Flail |
| Feebas | Splash, Tackle, Water Gun, Disarming Voice |
| Wimpod | Tackle, Struggle Bug, Aqua Jet, Defense Curl |

All begin at level 5. Train supporting Pokémon, carry recovery items and build a full party. Exclusive growth, forms and powers belong to your original individual, not every Pokémon of its species. The original cannot be transferred to another game.

## Starter setup

After choosing your partner, customize shininess, nature, IVs (0–31) and EVs (up to 252 per stat, 510 total). Cancel to preserve the generated partner. Return through **Starter Setup** on a PC; it can locate your original in the party or a PC Box. It is not a general Pokémon editor and does not grant levels, forms or story milestones.

Use **L/R** for pages, **Up/Down** for fields, **Left/Right** to change values, **START / Apply changes** to apply, and **B** to cancel.

## Growth and evolution

The original earns +1 to each calculated stat per level after level 5, plus an extra +1 at levels 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30. This caps at **+30 to each calculated stat at level 30**, retained through evolution and awakening. It is separate from base stats, EVs and IVs.

All three evolve at **level 30**. Feebas does not need Beauty or an evolution item for this custom path. [Compare their forms and ordinary moves](MOVES-AND-STATS.md).

## Powers

**Legend's Resolve** remains available throughout the original partner's journey. Once per battle, an otherwise lethal direct opposing hit that reaches its HP deals no HP damage; it fully heals, clears harmful personal conditions and negative stat stages, and raises Attack, Defense, Sp. Atk, Sp. Def and Speed by two stages. It protects one hit, not subsequent hits of a multi-hit move. Residual/self-inflicted damage and hits absorbed by Substitute do not trigger it. Switching does not reset it. Applicable ability suppression still matters.

| Line | Evolved power | Awakened power |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gyarados | **Rising Tyrant:** physical Water/Dragon damage +20% | **Tidal Sovereign:** Water/Dragon damage +30%, physical or special |
| Milotic | **Mystic Grace:** special Fairy/Dragon damage +20% | **Celestial Sovereign:** Fairy/Dragon damage +30%, physical or special |
| Golisopod | **Abyssal Bulwark:** ordinary incoming move damage reduced 25%; opposing Defense/Sp. Def drops blocked | **Leviathan's Aegis:** replaces Bulwark; damage reduced 40%, same drop protection, recovery of 1/16 max HP each turn |

When both offensive powers apply, their multipliers combine to 1.56×. Golisopod's reductions do not stack. Aegis recovery is at least 1 HP and respects Heal Block and suppression; Leftovers can also recover HP. Self-inflicted defensive drops still apply. Fixed-damage, one-hit-KO and residual damage retain their native rules.

Open **Summary → Powers** and use Up/Down to read Growth and power cards. On **Skills**, use **R** for IVs, **L** for EVs and **START** for calculated stats.

## Tideborn Awakening

<details><summary>Show the Lake of Rage unlock requirements</summary>

Defeat or catch the scripted red Gyarados at Lake of Rage. Either records the awakening unlock; running away or losing does not. Your original does not need to land the final hit. An evolved original in the party or a PC Box can awaken. If still young or temporarily unavailable, eligibility remains pending until it is ready and the game refreshes it. Evolve it and retrieve it from Day Care if needed.

Awakening does not replace your equipped moves. Use the original partner's free reminder to choose the newly available moves.

## Move management

Open your original partner's **party Summary → battle Moves page → A → A**. Choose **Relearn Move**, **Delete Move**, or **Reorder Moves**. The free reminder includes eligible moves unlocked through level, evolution, awakening or actual teaching history. TM/tutor compatibility alone does not unlock an untaught move.

Young-form moves remain available after evolution. Deletion requires confirmation and leaves at least one move; the ordinary last-needed-Surf safeguard also applies. Read every special-move warning before accepting a permanent change. These custom tools belong to the original starter.

## Blackthorn special training

Use the Blackthorn Special Training opportunities as they become available in the adventure. Teaching eligibility and the original partner's current form still apply. The ordinary compatibility lists are in [moves and stats](MOVES-AND-STATS.md); a listed move is not a free automatic grant.

## Training and everyday conveniences

The [training guide](TRAINING.md) explains Power items, free PC IV Training and **5-BP PP Max**. Options include indoor running, autorun, faster surfing/fishing, EXP settings and display/audio preferences. EXP Share and field-use conveniences follow their progression/settings requirements. Recommended challenge defaults enable reusable TMs and field-poison survival at 1 HP. Custom Fairy typing and modern physical/special categories are configured for this edition.

## Final Ascension

Raise your awakened original to earned level 100 for its final discovery. Its names, hidden mechanics and animations are deliberately omitted here. Read the in-game prompts carefully.
