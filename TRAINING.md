## EV Training Kit

Open the player's PC, then **Item Storage → Withdraw Item**. A new game starts with six of each item alongside the original Potion. Existing games receive the same kit once when they open Item Storage, including the bedroom PC.

| Item | Additional EVs per eligible defeat | Starting PC quantity |
|---|---|---:|
| Power Weight | +8 HP | 6 |
| Power Bracer | +8 Attack | 6 |
| Power Belt | +8 Defense | 6 |
| Power Lens | +8 Sp. Attack | 6 |
| Power Band | +8 Sp. Defense | 6 |
| Power Anklet | +8 Speed | 6 |

The entire kit must fit before anything is added. If storage is full, withdraw enough items and reopen Item Storage to retry. Existing items remain intact. The receipt is saved with the inventory, so withdrawing, selling, or using the kit does not grant it again.

Withdraw an item and give it to a Pokémon through the normal Bag/party menus. Equipping an item does not change EVs immediately. In eligible battles, it adds **8 EVs in its designated stat regardless of the defeated Pokémon's normal EV yield**. The defeated Pokémon's ordinary yield is still awarded separately; a Power item does not redirect that yield or restrict all gains to one stat.

For example, a Power Bracer holder defeating an opponent that normally awards 1 Defense EV gains **+8 Attack EVs and +1 Defense EV**, before modifiers and caps. Enabled Pokérus doubles both the ordinary yield and Power-item bonus; active and cured Pokérus qualify. That example becomes +16 Attack and +2 Defense with enabled Pokérus. If both sources target the same stat, they add together: Caterpie plus Power Weight grants 9 HP EVs, or 18 with enabled Pokérus, before caps.

You do not need to find a particular wild species just to receive the item's +8 bonus. For precise spreads, however, account for the opponent's ordinary EV yield: other stats can accumulate EVs too.

Existing limits remain **252 per stat and 510 total**. Power items retain their Speed penalty while held. EXP Share can distribute EVs to eligible inactive party members. The EXP multiplier changes EXP, not EV gains; intentional challenge restrictions still apply. These items do not assign or reset EVs automatically.

## IV Training

**IV Training is separate from EV training.** Power items add EVs through eligible battles; this free PC service changes selected IVs and leaves existing EVs unchanged.

Choose **IV Training** from the Pokémon Center PC's main menu. It is free and available before the League. Boxed Pokémon can be withdrawn first.

1. Select a party Pokémon and review its six IVs.
2. Choose **Maximize All IVs**, **Maximize Selected IVs**, or **Cancel**.
3. For selected training, toggle the stats to improve and review the selection.
4. Read the Hidden Power warning and explicitly confirm **Yes**. The initial choice is **No**.

The service sets the selected actual IVs to **31**. It accepts ordinary caught, traded, gifted, legendary, evolved, level 100 and valid custom Pokémon. Eggs and invalid records are excluded. Already-perfect values are a harmless no-op.

Species, OT, personality, shininess, nature, ability, gender, nickname, EXP, level, EVs, friendship, moves, PP, held item, status and custom progression/history are preserved. Derived stats are recalculated. A full-health Pokémon stays full; an injured Pokémon keeps the same damage taken; a fainted Pokémon remains fainted, including after boxing. If the selected Pokémon changes while the menu is open, the request is rejected without modifying it.

Changing IVs can change **Hidden Power's type**. This build uses its existing fixed base power of **60**. All six IVs at 31 produce Dark-type Hidden Power. Individual-stat selection lets the player avoid unwanted changes; the service does not alter Hidden Power's formula.

Starter setup remains a separate feature. IV Training does not change enemy trainers, the custom starters' balance, Awakening, Final Ascension or story progression.


## PP Max

**PP Max costs 5 BP** at the Battle Frontier Exchange Service Corner's **vitamin clerk**. It uses the existing PP rules; it is not a change to any move's base PP. Additional PP-shop convenience is not part of v1.0.
