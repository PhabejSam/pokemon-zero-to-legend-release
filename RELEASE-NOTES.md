# Pokémon: Zero to Legend v1.0 — release notes

**Official v1.0.** This release uses the owner-accepted RC1 game and patch bytes unchanged. It introduces no gameplay change relative to that candidate.

- A Heart & Soul 2.0 readaptation spanning Johto, Kanto, 16 Gyms and the journey to Red.
- Three original starter journeys: Feebas, Magikarp and Wimpod, with custom evolved designs, Tideborn Awakening, followers and dedicated Powers pages.
- A final earned level-100 discovery, kept hidden in release previews.
- Approved Zero to Legend title artwork, custom battle sprites, earlier signature techniques and inherited quality-of-life settings.
- Existing stabilization fixes, starter move tools, early Power-item access and free PC IV Training.
- Existing PP mechanics and inventories retained. PP Max costs **5 BP** at the Frontier vitamin clerk. Additional recovery-shop convenience is deferred beyond v1.0.

## Validation and limits

The owner reports completing Johto/Kanto/Red and passing exact-RC1 RG40XX H/VBA Next acceptance, including title, music, battles, abilities, existing progress, native Save and cold Continue. These are physical-device reports. Desktop checks separately cover the documented patch, save and selected facility/breeding/Contest/encounter paths. They are not exhaustive certification.

The remaining smart-AI timing assertions and optional safety/reward gaps are listed in [known limitations](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md). The owner explicitly accepts those residual risks for v1.0; failed assertions and unverified scopes remain unchanged. No new save corruption, permanent party/item loss or progression-blocking defect was reproduced in the recorded targeted checks; untested paths remain uncertain.

## Updating

Use your own backup and a copy of an existing same-edition battery save. Cold-launch and use native Continue, not an old emulator save state. Check trainer, location, party and inventory before saving. Do not overwrite a working original if Continue is absent or incorrect. Cross-edition DSi transfers and arbitrary old-version migrations are not certified. See [installation](INSTALL.md).
